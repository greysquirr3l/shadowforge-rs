<section class="sf-section">
	<h2 class="sf-section__title">Capabilities</h2>
	<p class="sf-section__lede">
		Ten steganographic techniques across image, audio, PDF, text, and corpus selection, with
		post-quantum protection and countermeasures for the attacks that actually matter against
		adversaries with nation-state budgets.
	</p>

	<div class="sf-grid">
		<a class="sf-card" href="/shadowforge-rs/architecture/crypto/">
			<p class="sf-card__title">Post-quantum cryptography</p>
			<p class="sf-card__body">
				ML-KEM-1024 key encapsulation and ML-DSA-87 signatures, both pure Rust NIST
				finalists. No liboqs, no CGo, no external crypto dependency to audit.
			</p>
			<p class="sf-card__meta">ml-kem · ml-dsa · argon2id · aes-256-gcm</p>
		</a>

		<a class="sf-card" href="/shadowforge-rs/architecture/bounded-contexts/">
			<p class="sf-card__title">PDF as a first-class context</p>
			<p class="sf-card__body">
				Content-stream least-significant-bit embedding, metadata watermarking, and a
				page-render pipeline — not a bolt-on. PDFs are the format sources actually
				exchange.
			</p>
			<p class="sf-card__meta">lopdf · pdfium-render</p>
		</a>

		<a class="sf-card" href="/shadowforge-rs/cli/embed-distributed/">
			<p class="sf-card__title">Distribution patterns</p>
			<p class="sf-card__body">
				1:1, 1:N, N:1, and N:M matrix distribution over Reed-Solomon K-of-N shards, so no
				single compromised carrier loses the payload.
			</p>
			<p class="sf-card__meta">reed-solomon-erasure · hmac-sha256</p>
		</a>

		<a class="sf-card" href="/shadowforge-rs/opsec/overview/">
			<p class="sf-card__title">Operational security</p>
			<p class="sf-card__body">
				Amnesiac mode writes nothing to disk, dual-payload deniability answers compulsion
				with a decoy, and dead drop mode posts to public services without a direct transfer.
			</p>
			<p class="sf-card__meta">amnesiac · deniable · dead drop · scrubber</p>
		</a>

		<a class="sf-card" href="/shadowforge-rs/threats/steganalysis/">
			<p class="sf-card__title">Countermeasures</p>
			<p class="sf-card__body">
				Adversarial embedding optimisation, camera-model fingerprint matching, and
				compression-survivable embedding calibrated against real platform pipelines.
			</p>
			<p class="sf-card__meta">adaptive · corpus · canary · timelock</p>
		</a>

		<a class="sf-card" href="/shadowforge-rs/threats/overview/">
			<p class="sf-card__title">Threat model first</p>
			<p class="sf-card__body">
				Compelled decryption, traffic analysis, endpoint compromise, legal pressure, and
				stylometric identification are documented as design constraints, not footnotes.
			</p>
			<p class="sf-card__meta">threat model · residual risks</p>
		</a>
	</div>
</section>
